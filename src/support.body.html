Support

Need help with boootie, the app or a pre-order? Most answers are below. For anything else, reply to your order confirmation email and a real person will get back to you.

Common questions

How do I set up boootie?
Install the boootie app, plug boootie into your phone's USB-C port, and follow the steps in the app to register your phone and connect it to your AI assistant.
How do I connect or change my AI assistant?
The app gives you a connection link for your phone. Add it to the assistant you want to use, such as Claude. To switch, remove the link from one assistant and add it to another.
How do I stop it?
Stop casting in the app or unplug boootie. Your phone stops answering the assistant immediately.
How do I delete my data?
boootie stores only a random phone id and hashed keys; to have them deleted, follow the steps in the Privacy Policy. Screenshots in your chat history are deleted in your AI provider's settings.
How do I get my deposit back?
Reply to your Stripe receipt email. Deposits are refunded in full, to the card you paid with, at any time before your order ships.
