The short version

How boootie works

You connect boootie to an AI assistant, such as Claude. While you are casting, the boootie app keeps an encrypted connection (TLS) open to boootie's relay server. When the assistant wants to see your screen or act on it, its request goes to the relay, the relay passes it to your phone, and your phone answers through the relay.

The connection is encrypted in transit, but not end to end: to forward a screenshot, the relay decrypts it, so for that moment it exists unencrypted in the relay's memory on Cloudflare's servers. It is not written to storage.

What data is involved

Passed through the relay, not stored

Screenshots
When the assistant asks to see your screen, which also happens after every action it takes, your phone captures the current screen, compresses it to a JPEG and sends it through the relay to the assistant. The image is held in the relay's memory only while that one request is in flight, then discarded. It is never saved, logged or viewed by boootie. Anything visible on your screen at that moment, such as messages, names or notifications, can appear in the screenshot.
Actions: taps, swipes and typed text
The assistant's instructions to tap, swipe, scroll or type travel through the relay to your phone the same way: forwarded, not stored. Typed text can include anything you asked the assistant to type.

Stored by boootie

Phone registration
For each phone: a random phone id, SHA-256 hashes of the phone's access key and connection link (not the key or link themselves), and the date the phone registered. We use these to let only your assistant reach your phone.
Request logs
Cloudflare's request logging is enabled for the relay. Each log entry records technical details such as the time, the request path and the response status. The request path contains your phone id and connection link secret, so these appear in the logs. Logs never contain screenshots or typed text. They are used to keep the relay running and to investigate faults, and they are deleted automatically when Cloudflare's log retention period ends.

Handled by your AI provider

Your conversation with your assistant, including what you ask it to do and the screenshots it receives, is between you and your AI provider. For example, Anthropic processes it for Claude under Anthropic's privacy policy, which decides how long it is kept and whether it is used to improve their models. Please review the settings and policy of the provider you connect.

When you visit boootie.com

Analytics
We count page views and button clicks with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you personally.
Where you came from
If you arrive from a link with a campaign tag (for example from a video), the tag is passed to the checkout so we can see which video led to a pre-order. It is kept in your browser for that visit only.

When you pre-order

Payment and order details
Deposits are processed by Stripe, which collects your name, email, billing details and card information under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email, country, the amount paid and the order status, and use them to fulfil and refund your order and to contact you about it. We never see your full card number.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process data to provide the service and orders you asked for (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), to keep the relay secure and working, including request logs (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)), and to meet legal obligations such as bookkeeping (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Service providers and recipients

  • Cloudflare hosts boootie's relay server as our processor. Screenshots, actions and request logs pass through its infrastructure as described above.
  • The AI provider you connect (for example Anthropic) receives the screenshots and results of actions your assistant asks for. It acts under its own terms and privacy policy, not on our behalf.
  • Stripe processes pre-order payments and refunds.
  • Vercel hosts boootie.com and provides cookieless analytics, as our processor.
  • Apple distributes the app; Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store downloads.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

International transfers

Cloudflare, Vercel, Stripe and AI providers such as Anthropic may process data outside Switzerland and the EU, including in the United States. Where that happens, transfers rely on adequacy decisions (such as the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Screenshots and actions: not stored by boootie; they exist only in memory while a single request is in flight.
  • Phone registration (random id, hashed keys, registration date): until you ask us to delete it or stop using boootie.
  • Relay request logs: until Cloudflare's log retention period ends.
  • Order and payment records: as long as needed for your order, then 10 years as Swiss accounting law requires.
  • Data held by your AI provider: according to that provider's policy and your settings with them.

Security

Connections between your phone, the relay and your assistant are encrypted in transit with TLS. Access keys and connection links are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. Your phone only answers requests while you are casting, and you can stop casting at any time.

Children

boootie is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
