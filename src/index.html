Can I get my deposit back?{{> chevron}}

Yes. In full, at any time before your order ships, no questions asked. Reply to your receipt email or write to {{supportEmail}} and we refund it to the card you paid with.

What can the AI see and do on my phone?{{> chevron}}

While it’s working on a task you gave it, boootie can see your screen and act on it: open apps, tap, type and scroll. It does what you asked and you watch it happen.

boootie is designed in Switzerland and built to Swiss data protection law and the EU’s GDPR. It only looks at your screen while doing something you asked, your data is never sold, and you can delete it anytime. We’ll publish the full details in plain language before launch.

Which AI does it use?{{> chevron}}

Your choice. Connect the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already have, or pick any other model you prefer. boootie is the hands; you choose the brain.

Does it need an app?{{> chevron}}

Probably a small one, for setup. We haven’t locked this down yet; we’ll tell you before launch.

Does it charge my phone?{{> chevron}}

No. boootie has no battery of its own. It has a second USB-C port, so you can plug your usual charger into boootie and your phone keeps charging while boootie is connected.
