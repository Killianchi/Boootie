Support

Need help with boootie, the app or a pre-order? Most answers are below. For anything else, reply to your order confirmation email and a real person will get back to you.

Common questions

How do I set up boootie?
Install the boootie app, plug boootie into your phone's USB-C port, and follow the steps in the app to pair it and connect your AI account.
How do I connect or change my AI provider?
In the app, open Settings → AI provider. You can sign in with a supported provider or switch to another model at any time.
How do I stop a task?
Tap Stop in the app or unplug boootie. It stops acting on your phone immediately.
How do I delete my data?
In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
How do I get my deposit back?
Reply to your Stripe receipt email. Deposits are refunded in full, to the card you paid with, at any time before your order ships.
